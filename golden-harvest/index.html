    FAQ

    Should I register through the Golden Harvest site?
    No — register through Little Saigon Krewe first. Clubs can register with Golden Harvest on their own. Golden Harvest is looking for at least 15 members per group. Our job is to combine smaller groups so the combined headcount meets that minimum.
    Is transportation provided?
    No. Groups arrange their own ride — drive, carpool, or take SacRT on their own. Little Saigon Krewe is not running a bus or a staffed light-rail escort. The 2025 parade packet had a SacRT free-ride coupon (not a krewe shuttle); look for whether 2026 repeats that.
    Do we perform along the whole route?
    No. Golden Harvest says parade teams may perform briefly — about 2–3 minutes — in front of the main stage and a second small stage. The rest of Capitol Mall is a walking march. Detailed route information comes about two weeks before the parade.
    Are there changing rooms?
    No. Golden Harvest does not provide changing rooms. Plan to arrive dress-ready. There is space on Capitol Mall for practice and warm-up before step-off.
    Is there a dress code?
    Not currently — no required uniform. The parade is televised, though, so dress in a way that shows your club or group identity (colors, shirts, banners, traditional wear if you like). Costume OK; traditional optional.
